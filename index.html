The Coimbatore Hydrology Story

The Journey of Living Water

Between the rainforest canopy of the Western Ghats and the taps of Peelamedu or Saravanampatti, water undertakes a hidden transformation through ancient rock. Here is the story of how water loses its balance—and how EcoTech restores it to living purity.

Chapter 01 🌧️

The Western Ghats Genesis

Every drop starts above the Silent Valley and Siruvani catchment. In its virgin atmospheric state, rain is naturally soft, sweet, and pure—water as nature created it.

Purity:100% Virgin Soft
TDS:< 35 ppm
Hardness:< 10 ppm
Untouched natural state
Chapter 02 ⛏️

The Deep Granite Shift

As water sinks 400 to 1,200 ft into Kongu charnockite fissures, it dissolves dense underground minerals: Calcium Carbonate (CaCO₃) and Magnesium. It looks crystal-clear, but carries heavy mineral weight.

State:Heavy Mineral Load
Borewell TDS:800 – 1,800 ppm
Hardness:450 – 950 ppm
Invisible calcification begins
Chapter 03 ⚠️

The Domestic Collision

The moment this hard groundwater meets electric geyser coils or room-temperature tap aerators, calcium crystallizes into stone. It coats heating elements, strips hair cuticle lipids, and stains bathroom tiles white.

Impact:Stone Scale & Hair Fall
Geyser Life:Reduced by 60%
Soap Waste:+50% extra detergent
Thousands spent in hidden repairs
